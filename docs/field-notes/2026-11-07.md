# Field notes - 2026-11-07

First real session on the target hardware: EOS 60D, EF100mm f/2.8 Macro USM,
fixed tripod, 100 mm.

## What was proven

- **A real frame plate-solved.** ASTAP returned `PLTSOLVD=T` on an 8.78 deg tall
  frame with only the D80 database installed. It cropped the centre and said so
  (`WARNING=Large FOV, use G05 database!`), but the centre, scale and position
  angle were usable. Those are what POINT consumes.
- **POINT worked on the body.** The marks and arrows had been gated on
  `camera.roll_deg` and `camera.pointing`, neither of which a 60D can report.
  After the solve they come from the solution's RA/Dec and PA, and the page
  draws the target circle, reticle, drift line and correction arrow.
- **FOCUS ran on real live view.** Lens drive, live-view HFD and the autofocus
  routine all worked after the fixes below.
- **RUN captured real frames.** The session output work was written afterwards
  and was not exercised live in this session.

## Fixed in `bec8451`

- Focus: both shoulders swept, one landing, the landing verified against the
  best measured sample, and every sample written to `focus.jsonl`.
- Rig profile: `reconcile()` separates the camera's current identity from the
  provenance of the geometry.
- Camera recovery: connect retries a body left busy by a killed process and
  forces EVF output off once a session exists; `ui.sh` shuts down through the
  app; camera errors persist to `ui.log`.
- Solve: 1 s exposure at the session ISO, previous ISO/Tv restored, refusal to
  change what cannot be restored, and no page refresh from the worker thread.
- Live view: pixel averaging (1/2/4/8, default 4) before HFD and display.
- POINT: solved pointing/PA fallback, alt/az advice in head terms, and the hint
  drawn on the frame.
- Auto re-solve: instruction grace + recheck slider + nudge + settle.
- Session output: `YYYY_MM_DD-HH-MM-TARGETCODE` under the SETUP output folder.

## Next: flats

Nothing produces a flat yet, although the stack already divides each frame by
one in sensor coordinates. Brief 15 section 7 is the design; the practical
checklist is:

- **At the end of the session**, optional and abortable. A leading step runs at
  the wrong focus and taxes the time-to-first-frame budget.
- **20-30 frames**, ISO matched to the lights, histogram 50-70%, 0.5-2 s each
  (about 30 s total).
- **Normalise by the median**, then **floor at about 0.2** before dividing: a
  dead pixel is near-zero in the flat and an unguarded divide produces NaNs.
- **Bin the master flat the same way the frames are binned**, and divide per
  frame in sensor coordinates **before** any warping. Correcting the
  accumulator is wrong twice over: the pattern is sensor-fixed, and the stack
  has already averaged vignetting down.
- **Source:** at 100 mm the entrance pupil is 22 mm, so a phone screen works.
  At 400 mm it is 71 mm; use a t-shirt and torch or a laptop screen.
- **Metadata matters:** ISO, aperture, focal length and focus position must
  travel with the flat. A silently mismatched flat is worse than no flat.
- **Invalidated** by any change to focus, zoom, aperture or dust.

Open questions: sky flat versus panel; where the flat step lives in the wizard
(`DONE` is the natural place); how the master is stored per session; and how
the offline restack verifies the match.

## Next: packaging

- **Windows onedir build.** Brief 09 measured a frozen PyInstaller onedir with a
  python-build-standalone interpreter; onefile adds seconds of extraction. The
  MSVC runtime is bundled, so there is no VC++ redistributable prerequisite.
- **Ship an unmodified `EDSDK.dll`** as part of the app under Canon's SDK
  agreement. It must never enter git. Stop depending on the copies borrowed
  from N.I.N.A. or Lightroom.
- **NiceGUI is a local HTTP server**, so the installer has to deal with a port
  and a possible firewall prompt. The browser UI is the shipping shape; no
  native window is required.
- **No new copyleft.** PySide6 stays gone; Siril and ASTAP stay subprocesses.
- **Session persistence is a prerequisite.** Today a server restart builds a
  fresh `SessionState` and returns to PREFLIGHT, which is why every deploy
  tonight cost the wizard. A shippable app must load the saved session.
- Keep the Windows console ASCII, and keep `tools/*.bat` on CRLF.

## Next: Raspberry Pi

- **The blocker is the camera backend, not the UI.** libgphoto2 replaces EDSDK
  behind the existing `Camera`/`FocusDriver` interfaces; `manualfocusdrive`
  already has the same three-tier structure the motion planner expects.
  Watch libgphoto2 issue #767: it alternates result/`None` between calls.
- **Resolution for live stacking is about 2K.** Brief 14 measured roughly
  0.25 s/frame on an SBC at 2K with the predicted-rotation prior, against
  several seconds at full resolution.
- **Solve every N frames**, not every frame: a solve is a subprocess spawn plus
  a large image read.
- **Storage:** an SSD, not an SD card, for saved frames.
- **Power:** separate rails for SBC and camera (dummy battery); the shutter is
  the brownout risk. The phone is usually the runtime limit.
- **Focal-length ceiling** on the portable build is set by the camera's USB
  link, not by SBC compute.

Open questions: live-view frame rate over gphoto2 on Pi-class hardware, ASTAP
solve time on the SBC (sets N), and whether the 60D exposes the same focus
drive over PTP as it does over EDSDK.

## Loose ends from this session

- **Session state does not survive a restart.** `ui.app` creates a fresh
  `SessionState()` and never loads the saved one. This is the reason each
  deploy reset the wizard.
- **ASTAP needs G05 at 100 mm.** D80 tops out at 5.14 deg and an 8.78 deg frame
  is cropped. Solving still works, but the verified envelope shrinks. Install
  G05 (and W08 if wider lenses are ever supported).
- **The last two batches are committed but were not run live:** the auto-solve
  instruction window and the session output folder. Validate them on the next
  clear night.
- **Starting values to tune in the field:** 2 s settle, 5 s recheck, 2.5 levels
  for nudge arming, and 4-frame live-view averaging.
